A short introduction

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. I received my Ph.D. from the same school in 2024, supported by a full NTU Ph.D. scholarship. Before that, I received my M.Eng. from the State Key Laboratory of Robotics and System at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), supervised by Academician Hong Liu and Prof. Minghe Jin, and my B.Eng. from the School of Mechatronics Engineering, HIT.

My research combines physical imaging models with deep learning to solve inverse problems in biomedical imaging. Most of my work is on acoustic-resolution photoacoustic microscopy (AR-PAM), where I develop degradation models, deep and plug-and-play priors, generative and diffusion-based reconstruction, and hardware-efficient implementations for real-time handheld systems.

Research

Projects I have led or worked on, most recent first.

Ultrasound Imaging Enhancement

Project Leader · CICS Laboratory, NTU · Oct 2023 – present

  • Proposed SonoSDE, an unsupervised score-based diffusion framework that enhances handheld ultrasound images toward high-end clinical quality without paired training data.
  • Designed physics-guided experts for SonoSDE: a Realistic Expert that matches frequency spectrum, speckle statistics and texture, and a training-free Faithful Expert based on Log-Gabor filters and phase congruency that preserves anatomy.
  • Developed an unsupervised latent diffusion model for real-time enhancement of portable medical ultrasound images.

Related: IEEE TIP (under review)

Handheld Photoacoustic Imaging Enhancement

Project Leader · CICS Laboratory, NTU · Oct 2023 – present

  • Developed a real-time enhancement platform for a handheld photoacoustic imaging system, running a ternary-quantized MultiResU-Net on a FeRAM crossbar array for in-memory computing.
  • Proposed a fast crossbar charging model that accounts for line and parasitic conductance, enabling hardware-aware training.
  • Validated the platform in vivo: lateral resolution improved from 850 µm to 78 µm, network inference finished within microseconds, and 30 Hz real-time imaging was kept.

Related: IEEE TBCAS 2025 · ISCAS 2025

Model-Based Photoacoustic Imaging Enhancement

Project Leader · CICS Laboratory, NTU · Sep 2021 – May 2025

  • Developed the degradation model of acoustic-resolution photoacoustic microscopy.
  • Designed a deep CNN denoiser prior and its optimization scheme to enhance imaging results.
  • Designed a joint group sparsity and deep prior model with an adaptive optimization scheme.

Related: Photoacoustics 2023 · IEEE TIP 2025 · BioCAS 2023

Learning-Based Photoacoustic Imaging Enhancement

Project Leader · CICS Laboratory, NTU · Jul 2019 – Sep 2023

  • Developed the degradation model of photoacoustic imaging for training data synthesis.
  • Used GAN-based learning to achieve realistic, high-resolution imaging enhancement.
  • Employed a Dual Branch Gated Fusion Network to improve imaging resolution and speed together.

Related: IEEE TMI 2022 · IEEE TCI 2025 · ISCAS 2021 · ISCAS 2022 · BioCAS 2022
